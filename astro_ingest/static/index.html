A quick first pass using the ASIAIR's own thumbnails: leave out anything obviously bad (clouds, trailing, dawn). Every light frame you keep is scored in detail on Review. Frames not yet on the NAS are below, oldest first (left to right, top to bottom); click a thumbnail to leave it out, or leave out a whole set. Stage reads each selected frame from the ASIAIR once over Wi-Fi, checksums it on the way in, and saves it to the staging share. Light frames are then scored for Review, and Copy & verify copies from staging at disk speed. Nothing is deleted from the ASIAIR until Clean up. If the connection drops, press Stage again to pick up where it stopped.

Each light frame is scored against the rest of its group (star count, FWHM, eccentricity, SNR, sky background), the same way astro-stacker reviews lights. Red-bordered frames are recommended not to be ingested; click one to see why, and keep it if you disagree. Nothing is copied until you approve it on Copy & verify.

Decisions

Frames the app can't file on its own wait here for an answer: a target it doesn't recognize, flats with no lights to go with, a very small group of lights, a damaged copy already on the NAS, or frames missing from a session that's already there. Where a safe default exists it's pre-selected; nothing under a decision is copied or deleted until it has an answer.

Clean up the device
loading
0%

Deletes from the device only what you tick; nothing is ticked for you. Frames whose NAS copy a checksum proves identical are marked recommended: copied by this app, or checked by Verify, which reads each older frame from the device once. Everything else is called out. Each .fit goes with its _thn.jpg. Right before deleting, the device is listed again (every file must be unchanged, and nothing written in the last 15 minutes) and each NAS copy is re-checked. Nothing on the NAS is touched.
