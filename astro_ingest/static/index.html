Looks for capture devices on your home network (today: ZWO ASIAIR). Searching or connecting only logs in as guest and lists the top of the device's share; nothing on it is opened, changed or deleted.

What's on the ASIAIR
not scanned

Scanning only reads: it lists every file and reads frame headers in Autorun/ and Plan/, on the ASIAIR's internal storage and, when present, its SD card and a USB drive. Nothing is written or deleted.

Choose what to stage from the device

A quick first pass using the ASIAIR's own thumbnails: leave out anything obviously bad (clouds, trailing, dawn). Every light frame you keep is scored in detail on Review. Frames not yet on the NAS are below, oldest first (left to right, top to bottom); click a thumbnail to leave it out, or leave out a whole set. A frame counts as already on the NAS when a file with the same name and size is anywhere on the Astronomy share: ASIAIR file names include the capture time to the second, so that's the same frame (Clean up still checks the contents before anything is deleted). Left-out frames are never read; they stay on the ASIAIR and are offered for deletion, called out, on Clean up.

Stage reads each selected frame from the ASIAIR once over Wi-Fi, checksums it on the way in, and saves it to the staging share. Light frames are scored when you open Review, and Copy & verify copies from staging at disk speed. Nothing is deleted from the ASIAIR until Clean up. If the connection drops, press Stage again to pick up where it stopped.

Each light frame is scored against the rest of its group (star count, FWHM, eccentricity, SNR, sky background), the same way astro-stacker reviews lights. Red-bordered frames are recommended not to be ingested; click one to see why, and keep it if you disagree. Nothing is copied until you approve it on Copy & verify.

Decisions

Frames the app can't file on its own wait here for an answer: a target it doesn't recognize, flats with no lights to go with, a very small group of lights, a damaged copy already on the NAS, or frames missing from a session that's already there. Where a safe default exists it's pre-selected; nothing under a decision is copied or deleted until it has an answer.

Clean up the device

Deletes only the files you tick; nothing is ticked for you. Each .fit goes with its _thn.jpg. Right before deleting, the device is listed again: files that changed, or that were written in the last 15 minutes (a capture in progress), are skipped, and every NAS copy is checked again. Nothing on the NAS is touched.
